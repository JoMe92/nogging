# Code of Conduct

Nogging is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and specific.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Focus criticism on ideas and observable behavior, not people.
- Protect private information and follow responsible disclosure practices.
- Accept moderation decisions made to protect the community.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project
spaces or when representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact
method on their GitHub profile. Do not disclose another person's private
information in an issue. The maintainer may edit or remove content, lock
threads, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with
fairness to everyone involved.

Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
